# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

This policy covers the Chrome extensions published from this repository:

- **YouTube Picture-in-Picture**
- **YouTube Auto Skip**
- **Transaction Info Copier**

## Summary

These extensions run entirely on your device. They do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or usage information. They do not connect to any external servers, analytics services, or advertising networks, and they contain no remote code.

## What is stored

The only data any of these extensions keeps is its own on/off settings (for example, whether the picture-in-picture button is shown or whether automatic picture-in-picture is enabled). These settings are saved with Chrome's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which means Chrome may synchronise them between your own signed-in Chrome profiles. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is ever sent to the developer.

## Permissions

- **youtube.com host access** (YouTube extensions): needed to add controls to the YouTube player and to keep already-open YouTube tabs working after the extension is updated. The extensions do not read or modify any other website.
- **storage**: needed to remember your settings.
- **scripting**: used only to load the extension's own bundled scripts into YouTube tabs that were already open when the extension was installed or updated.
- **activeTab / clipboardWrite** (Transaction Info Copier): used only when you click the extension to copy text from the current page to your clipboard.

## Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The extension popups contain a link to a donation page; nothing is loaded from that page unless you choose to click the link.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
